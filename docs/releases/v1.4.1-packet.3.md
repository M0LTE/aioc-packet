## What's new in 1.4.1-packet.3

- **9600 baud receive now works with the UV-K5.** The radio's receive audio rolls off below about 128 Hz, and the phase of that roll-off stops 9600 baud packet decoding: on the bench, live on hardware, 0 of 60 frames decoded from the radio's audio. A new receive equaliser undoes it inside the AIOC, so it works with any modem: with it, 60 of 60 on pdn-soundmodem, its offline decoder and Direwolf alike, on the same bench. (bench/EQ.md also quotes 0 of 70 and 70 of 70: those are earlier recordings run through a model of the filter.) 1200 and 3600 baud are unchanged. It is on by default, runs while the host records at 48000 Hz, and adds 7.0 ms of delay to received audio. With another radio, switch it off with `tools/aioc_eq.py rx off --store`.
- **Audio is now handled in 1 ms blocks** (by DMA) instead of one interrupt per sample. That leaves the AIOC plenty of headroom with both equalisers running while a modem plays and records at once (38% of the processor, measured), and adds 1 ms of delay each way.
- **USB reports are no longer sent from interrupts.** Carrier detect and the IN1/IN2 inputs reported to the host from inside an interrupt, which could collide with the USB stack's own work (upstream behaviour). They are sent from the main loop now.
- **Status register fix**: the PTT, virtual PTT and virtual COS states now show in register 0xC0, where their definitions put them. Upstream wrote them into 0xD0, over the recording mute bits and the play and record states. Only matters to software that reads those registers.
- **`aioc_eq.py diag`** says why the AIOC last restarted (watchdog, fault, host request), with a fault record that survives the restart, and how busy the processor is. Useful if anything ever goes wrong.
- **Upgrading with `-keep-settings`**: the receive equaliser comes on unless your settings were stored by a release that has it. The transmit equaliser behaves as before.
